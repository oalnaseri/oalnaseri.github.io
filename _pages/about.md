I excel in both teaching and industry. I balance my role as a lecturer at DHBW Ravensburg with leading R&D in ML/AI Algorithms for automotive sensor fusion at ZF Friedrichshafen AG, a leader in automotive technology. My expertise spans mobile/optical communication and cutting-edge autonomous vehicle systems, including the application of (Quantum) machine learning algorithms.

I boast a strong background in engineering, with a Ph.D. in Optical Communication Engineering and over 12 years of experience in the mobile communications sector. Furthermore, I have 8 years of experience in embedded system architecture for autonomous cars.
